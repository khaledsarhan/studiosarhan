I’m a photographer based in Vienna, currently completing the Photo Expert Diploma at the LIK Akademie für Foto und Design.

My interest in photography grew on the road. I was always taking pictures on my travels and road trips, and in 2016 I bought my first camera, which has come with me on every trip since.

I’m drawn to landscapes, to stillness and to motion, and to the details that are easy to walk past. Most of my work is in black and white, where light, shape and contrast carry the picture.

This site is where I gather that work while I keep learning. If you’d like to talk about a photograph, a collaboration, or just say hello, I’d love to hear from you.
