Everyday life moves through and rests inside the places we build. A gull cuts across a blurred wall of apartment blocks; a dog lies in the dust while a car blurs by; gulls scatter around a van passing by the sea; a man sits alone, absorbed, beneath a grand museum and a stormy sky. Through motion blur, selective color and quiet pauses, the series sets the still against the fast, and asks which of them we really notice.
