Neighbours that were never meant to match. A baroque church portal, all scrolls, urns and a white angel, leans against a plain gabled house with four square windows. A domed imperial museum rises behind a flat modern sign and a concrete bench, where a man sits between the two. The series looks for these meetings in the city, where ornament stands beside simplicity and old beside new, and lets each side make the other easier to see.
