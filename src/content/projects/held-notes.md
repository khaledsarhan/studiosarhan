A quiet study of an instrument between performances. Shot close and in black and white, the series follows the grain of the wood, the line of the strings, the curve of the f-holes and the pages of music around them. Nothing is played here. The frames hold the moment before sound, when the instrument is all texture, shadow and possibility.
