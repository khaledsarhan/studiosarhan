I’m Khaled Sarhan, a photographer currently completing the Photo Expert Diploma at the LIK Akademie für Foto und Design in Vienna.

Photography has interested me for a long time, but it really started on the road. On my travels and road trips I was always taking pictures, and in 2016 I bought my first camera, which has come with me on every trip since.

I’m drawn to landscapes and to motion: the wide, quiet view of a city from a hillside, and the moment something passes through the frame, like a gull crossing a wall of apartment blocks or a car blurring past a dog at rest. My series move between the two, mostly in black and white, looking for light, distance and stillness.

This site is where I gather that work while I keep learning. If you’d like to talk about a photograph, prints or a collaboration, I’d love to hear from you.
